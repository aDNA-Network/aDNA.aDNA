## Fields projects kept adding

Several projects started from the templates and then added the same fields by hand: which class
of model runs a mission, what it was budgeted, what it actually cost, what it is waiting on and
what must finish first. v8.14 puts those fields in the templates, so a new project starts with
them. One budget-actuals field is named, because projects had been recording the same number
under different names.

## Two questions before a gate

A mission's acceptance criteria can each look fine and still contradict one another: a method
that cannot produce the evidence its test asks for. The mission template now asks the author to
read every method against every test and record which pairs were read. The campaign template asks
the same of its checks in another form: a check must be shown to go red on purpose before anyone
trusts it green, and must say *cannot verify* rather than pass when it did not reach what it
names.

## Specification pages at v2.6

The specification pages on this site are generated from the standard. They had stayed at v2.5
after the standard moved to v2.6, and the script that writes them took its version number from a
fixed value rather than from the standard. It now reads the version from the standard's own
title, and the pages show v2.6.
