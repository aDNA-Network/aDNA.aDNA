# site_ourworldindata — Our World in Data

> **The structural-trust north star for a public-good property.** Credibility is built by *architecture* — metrics of content, named scholars, source-per-chart, license and funding disclosure — never by reach claims. The closest register model for aDNA's "context as democratic public good." Headless capture 2026-08-16 (`campaign_haussmann/evidence/dossier/captures/ourworldindata/`).

## Captured (rubric)

- **Hero (capture):** navy band, 13-word mission ("Research and data to make progress against the world's largest problems") with a **yellow accent on the key phrase**, a **centered search bar with example queries**, and four **content-metric pills** — 14,071 charts · 126 topics · 29 data explorers · 501 articles — plus "All free: open access and openly licensed." **The metrics count the shelf, not the audience** — no visitor/vanity numbers anywhere.
- **Trust stack, structural:** every chart carries **source attribution** (World Bank, UN FAO, Global Carbon Budget…) + **update recency** ("Data update – 13 days ago"); a nonprofit card ("We are a non-profit — all our work is free to use and openly licensed") with Donate; footer = Global Change Data Lab, UK charity number, **CC BY**, GitHub.
- **Named humans everywhere:** bylines on every artifact (Hannah Ritchie, Pablo Rosado, Esteban Ortiz-Ospina…), a hiring card with a team photo — people are the trust surface.
- **Charts are the imagery:** zero stock photos; article thumbnails ARE small charts. Daily "**Data Insights**" (dated bite-size chart + commentary + byline) make an archive feel alive on a daily clock.
- **IA:** popular-pages rail → featured data (heavily hyperlinked prose) → explorers → a full ~80-link topic taxonomy in 11 buckets. Density high but bucketed; light-only; serif headline + sans body. Tonal ~35 — mission-forward, register sober.

## Lift for aDNA

- **The one thing to steal: content-metrics-as-hero-proof.** aDNA's honest analogs — N vaults · N graphs · N skills · N ADRs — are shelf metrics that scale from day one, the exact escape from the HF scale-mismatch ([[site_huggingface]]) that also beats Val Town's approach for a *standards* property.
- **Byline + source + update-date on every artifact** — aDNA's frontmatter (`last_edited_by`, `updated`, provenance) already carries this; Haussmann's credibility phase should *render* it.
- **Artifact-as-imagery:** graphs/registry cards as thumbnails, never decoration.
- Funding/independence disclosure as a trust feature, stated plainly.

## Avoid

- The **density ceiling** — ~10 sections + an 80-link directory suits a 14k-chart archive; the homepage 5 ±1 budget holds ([[front_page_doctrine]] §2). Take the *stack*, not the sprawl.
- Light-only; no install/product surface to imitate.
