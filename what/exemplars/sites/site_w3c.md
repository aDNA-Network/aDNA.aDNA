# site_w3c — W3C (w3.org)

> **The institutional register done with real design-system craft** — proof that a standards body can look governed *and* designed. The closest register-peer for aDNA-as-standard; inspected home (headless capture 2026-08-16, `campaign_haussmann/evidence/dossier/captures/w3c/`) + the `/TR/` standards index.

## Captured (rubric)

- **Hero:** "Making the web work" (4 words) + a ~30-word **definition-as-hero**: "The W3C develops standards and guidelines to help everyone build and enjoy a web based on the principles of accessibility, internationalization, privacy and security" — with **every principle inline-hyperlinked**. No pitch; a definition whose load-bearing terms are doors.
- **Sections (7):** hero → member-stakeholder logos (explicitly "rotated randomly") → TPAC event (custom illustrated poster) → web standards → get involved → latest news → footer.
- **News IS process:** the news cards are maturity-stage events — "Updated W3C Recommendation: ARIA in HTML," "Group Note," "W3C Invites Implementations of WebTransport." The process vocabulary carries the aliveness signal.
- **`/TR/` index (deep page):** "1234 reports across 288 families" under a **status × tag facet system** (Draft/Candidate/Standard + Note/Statement/Registry classes; 19 topic tags), per-entry metadata (status badge · date · history link · deliverer group · translations), pre-built queries ("Standards only," "everything tagged Accessibility").
- **Visual (capture):** light-only; one navy accent; outlined-pill buttons; alternating image/text bands; a **carousel pause button** (a11y affordance shipped at hero level); i18n links (日本語/简体中文) in the top bar. Institutional but unmistakably art-directed — the TPAC poster is genuinely playful illustration inside one contained card.
- **Community:** org logos only; no named humans on home. Tonal ~20.

## Lift for aDNA

- **The one thing to steal: definition-as-hero with inline-linked principles.** aDNA's standard home can open with a tight definition whose key terms (context graph · federation · triad) each link to their concept page — credibility and IA in one sentence.
- **Process-vocabulary-as-news** — aDNA's changelog/releases read as standard events ("v2.5 ratified," "ADR-045 accepted"), which *is* the W3C aliveness pattern.
- **The `/TR/` facet triad (status × tag × family)** — the reference model for a vault/spec registry at hundreds-of-items scale.
- Ship the pause affordance on anything that moves (a11y as visible craft).

## Avoid

- **Committee-neutral voice** — nothing on the page wants anything; aDNA keeps its movement claim (Hermes pole) above the institutional plumbing.
- Org-logos-only community — refused per who-not-how-many ([[site_valtown]]).
