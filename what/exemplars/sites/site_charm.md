# site_charm — Charm (charm.sh → charm.land)

> **The most revolutionary voice in the corpus (75) — and it survives professionalism.** A terminal-tooling company whose whole property is candy-saturated whimsy, held credible by hard proof set directly beside it. Headless capture 2026-08-16 (`campaign_haussmann/evidence/dossier/captures/charm/`; note: charm.sh now 301s to **charm.land**).

## Captured (rubric)

- **Hero:** "We make the command line glamorous." (6 words) under a full-bleed glossy 3D lavender-blob cloudscape on a saturated purple field; flagship card ("Remember your first Crush?") pairs a pixel-heart mascot with a terminal panel.
- **The mascot program (the lesson):** every product — Bubble Tea, Huh, Lip Gloss, Wish, Glamour, Bubbles, Log, Harmonica, Mods, Gum, Glow, Skate — has **its own 3D character in ONE render language** (same gloss, palette, lighting). Personality is a *component system*: card = mascot slot + name + **GitHub-star pill** + tag pills + one factual line. (Correcting the HTML read: star counts ARE displayed — screenshot beat fetch.)
- **The counterweight:** "**Industrial grade.**" — a white logo wall (GitHub, Azure, AWS, NVIDIA, Shopify…) + "lives in over 25,000 applications, … business-critical infrastructure" placed mid-whimsy. Proof sits adjacent to play, never replaced by it.
- **Voice:** "This is fine. A happy, powerful little logger." · "Make your shell scripts pop!" · footer easter egg `haters > /dev/null™` — jokes live in taglines and margins; the facts stay straight.
- **Theme:** not dark-vs-light but **color-native** — one saturated brand field is the theme; cream footer wave closes it. Dense-but-ordered (9 sections); "We love open source." as a commitment section.
- **Gaps:** no humans anywhere (mascots substitute); no install commands on-site (outsourced to GitHub).

## Lift for aDNA

- **The one thing to steal: whimsy as a governed component system with proof adjacent** — one illustration language, one slot per artifact (mascot + star pill + tags + one-liner), and an "industrial grade" credibility block beside it. This is the systematization recipe the Ghibli-pixel question needs (see [[site_panic_playdate]] for the containment variant).
- **Voice in the margins:** personality carried by taglines/easter eggs while data stays factual — compatible with the ~55/45 dial.
- Per-item star/usage pills = the who-uses-this proof at card level.

## Avoid

- **Full-field saturated takeover** — the purple owns *every* pixel; at aDNA's docs/standards register this would fight legibility and the credibility pole ([[site_w3c]], OWID). Charm sells tools; aDNA carries a standard.
- Mascots-instead-of-humans and install-outsourced-to-GitHub both invert aDNA doctrine (named humans; command-in-hero, [[site_bun]]).
