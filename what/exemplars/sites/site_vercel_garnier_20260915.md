## Lift for aDNA

[I] Use deliberate hierarchy and different compositions for different jobs: mechanism, evidence and participation should not all be identical cards. Keep one clear first-task action with a quieter source-reading alternative, using aDNA's existing tokens. This is a proposed application to GARNIER, not a claim that this site's visual choices caused successful outcomes.

## Avoid

[I] The writing-guidelines AVOID rule concerns marketing claims in technical docs. Borrow hierarchy, not slogans, logo walls, unmeasured scale claims or a large brand mark ahead of the explanation. Do not import this homepage's copy into reference material.
