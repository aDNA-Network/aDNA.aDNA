# site_quanta — Quanta Magazine

> **Proof that an illustration-forward visual voice and scientific credibility can be the same property.** Simons-funded science journalism whose signature is commissioned art per story — wonder in the visuals, rigor in the text. The credibility-pole answer to the [[site_charm]]/[[site_panic_playdate]] whimsy question. Judged from HTML only [I] — outside the 5-capture budget; deep page: the Computer Science section.

## Captured (rubric)

- **Masthead:** ~10-word public-service tagline ("Illuminating basic science and math research through public service journalism"); the *hero is the lead story* — headline + portrait/art, not marketing.
- **The art program (the lesson):** **commissioned illustration/photography per story, one editorial art direction across all of it** — artist credits embedded (filenames literally carry "cr-Celsius-Pictor"); the story thumbnail and the article header share the same artwork. A *slot model*: the frame is fixed, the art rotates, the style is house-governed.
- **Named humans, double layer:** every piece = **byline → contributor page** (writers as durable identities) *plus* the credited artist — journalism and craft both attributed.
- **Independence as furniture:** "An editorially independent publication supported by the Simons Foundation" + the explicit "our articles do not reflect the views of the foundation" — funding disclosure repeated in the footer, load-bearing for trust.
- **IA:** discipline-first nav (Physics / Mathematics / Biology / CS) with a long secondary tail (Podcasts, Videos, Explainers…); topic tags under headlines; section pages = 4 featured + Most Read rail + topic filter. Sections framed exploratorily ("What's up in Computer Science").
- **Tone:** "a foundational result," "never-before-seen view" — awe carried by nouns, not adjectival hype. Light-only; medium-high density. Tonal ~40.

## Lift for aDNA

- **The one thing to steal: the governed art slot — one house illustration program, applied per-artifact in a fixed frame, always credited.** This is the credibility-register ceiling for adna.network's Ghibli-pixel style: per-surface art *inside* a stable frame, artist/pipeline credited, register intact (dossier ruling input).
- **Byline → contributor-page** promotion: aDNA's personas/operators as durable pages the registry links to.
- **Independence/funding disclosure as standing footer furniture** — pairs with [[site_ourworldindata]]'s nonprofit stack for aDNA's public-good claim.

## Avoid

- The **editorial cost structure** — commissioned art per story presumes a staffed art desk; aDNA must template or generate (VisualDNA/ComfyUI pipeline) rather than hand-craft per page.
- Nav-tail sprawl (11+ items) — the 5 ±1 and breadth-first rules hold ([[front_page_doctrine]]).
