# site_panic_playdate — Playdate (Panic)

> **The test case for "can a playful visual voice carry a whole property" — answer: yes, by containment.** A pixel-art game console sold with full institutional craft (spec tables, education program, credits, help). **Method note:** `panic.com/playdate` returns 404; the property lives at **play.date** — captured there headless 2026-08-16 (`campaign_haussmann/evidence/dossier/captures/panic_playdate/`).

## Captured (rubric)

- **Hero:** ~23 words — "It's a new, tiny handheld game system with a bunch of brand-new games. **We made Playdate just for fun.**" — the thesis is a *reason*, not a feature; price ("$229") and "Order now" sit in the fold.
- **The containment system (the lesson):** the **pixel art never touches the chrome.** It lives in *content slots* — the 1-bit device screen, game covers, catalog thumbnails. The chrome is a rigorous flat system: **signal-yellow field** (~60% of the page) color-blocked against black / white / teal / purple section bands, with one typographic rule — **short sentences with periods as headings** ("The System." "The Crank." "The Idea.").
- **Institutional spine inside the whimsy:** a real spec table flanked by line-art device diagrams; a season model (24 games, 2/week); **per-game developer credits inline** + a full credits page; SDK + Pulp maker for user games; an education program; transparent pricing repeated ("All for just $229," cover $34).
- **Voice:** "Is it a gimmick? Nah. … Is it really fun? Yes yes yes." · "a delicious electronic ice cream sandwich" · "peppy little processor" — personality in copy, discipline in structure.
- **Theme:** single brand theme (yellow IS the theme; no dark mode). 16 sections — the longest page in the corpus, held together by the color-band rhythm. Headless note: lazy-loaded game-roster media rendered empty [I].

## Lift for aDNA

- **The one thing to steal: pixel art in slots, system in the chrome.** The community/game-covers fill a fixed artifact slot while type+color carry the property — the direct systematization model for adna.network's Ghibli-pixel style (vault cards / hero panel as the "screen," Tokyo-Night system as the chrome). See the dossier ruling.
- **Sentence-with-period headings** — a cheap, ownable typographic voice compatible with the ~55/45 dial.
- **Credits-as-content:** every artifact names its maker inside the playful frame — who-not-how-many ([[site_valtown]]) executed at full whimsy.

## Avoid

- **16 sections / repeating commerce CTAs** — a hardware product's shape, triple the 5 ±1 homepage budget ([[front_page_doctrine]] §2).
- Single-color takeover without a dark variant — aDNA's docs surfaces keep the dual theme.
