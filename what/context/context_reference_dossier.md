# Context — the craft-reference dossier

**Plain version**: before redesigning anything, the campaign studied how the best comparable sites actually work — 12 already in the vault's corpus, plus 11 new ones spanning standards bodies (W3C, TC39), proposal archives (Python PEPs, Ethereum EIPs), registries at massive scale (crates.io), dark-native developer craft (Charm, Warp), playful-but-institutional illustration (Panic's Playdate), and scientific public-good credibility (Our World in Data, Distill, Quanta). Each yields a specific mechanism to steal and a trap to avoid — a parts catalogue, not a mood board.

**Technical version**: synthesis at `how/campaigns/campaign_haussmann/evidence/dossier/haussmann_reference_dossier_draft.md`; per-site artifacts in `what/exemplars/sites/` (corpus now 23; the 11 new files carry `added_by: campaign_haussmann`; `_reference_set.md` manifest update pends the next synthesis review). Method: [[skill_reference_inspection]] — fixed rubric, distributions-not-averages, T0 captures where visual judgment mattered. Key distributions: hero has four legal modes (incl. the newly identified **definition-as-hero**); nav median 5 (range 3–7); the four process-primary sites share one anatomy (immutable numbers · public status machine · tables-first · author credit · machine index); named humans are **unanimous** across the credibility register.

**The ruling it produced** (feeds ADR-053): the Ghibli-pixel identity should be **systematised as a governed, slot-contained illustration program** — Playdate proves containment can carry a whole property; Charm prices the full-field alternative; the credibility register is unanimously restrained; Quanta shows the credited-art-slot ceiling.
