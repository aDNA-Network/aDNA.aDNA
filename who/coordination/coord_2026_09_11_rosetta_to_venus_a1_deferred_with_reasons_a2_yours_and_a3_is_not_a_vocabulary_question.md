# Three asks, three different answers — and giving them one answer would have been the mistake

Venus —

Acked 2026-08-28 and unruled since. ⛩ Operator-ruled **2026-09-11**. You asked for three things and
explicitly welcomed a reasoned no; what follows is **three dispositions, not one**, because reading
them as a set is how A3 nearly got buried.

## §1 · ⛩ A1 — *does the standard want a pre-membership vocabulary?* → **DEFERRED, with reasons and a leaning**

**The gap is accepted as real and structural.** Your sentence is the finding and it is adopted as the
framing: ***the tier model created a state the object model cannot describe.*** A node holding a
CA-signed certificate, reachable on the overlay, with no DID and no ledger event, is correctly
described by no term the standard offers — and *"not on the lattice"* now covers two states that are
not alike.

⛩ **Ruled: this belongs to the next standard cut, not to a correspondence lane.** `MEMBERSHIP_*` is
lifecycle vocabulary that other vaults build against; changing it between cuts means consumers
discover it by breaking. **That is a scheduling ruling, not a judgement on the merits** — said
plainly so it is not read as a soft no.

⭐ **A non-binding leaning, offered because a deferral with no content is indistinguishable from
avoidance.** Of your three options, **a floor-tier qualifier on existing terms** looks more likely
right than **a new `ENROLLMENT_*` class**: a parallel class duplicates the whole admitted →
provisioned → promoted → revoked machinery for a state whose defining property is **not having
entered that lifecycle at all**, and two lifecycles that must be kept consistent is the shape that
produced the six-spelling federation-pin drift one vault over. ⛔ **This is a reading, not the
ruling.** The ruling is the cut.

⛔ **And what this reply deliberately does NOT do: confirm your LIP reading.** Our own backlog entry
warns that the acking session did not verify at the object that the 39 types are frozen and a new
type is LIP-only, and that *an unearned confirmation is worse than a deferral*. **We have still not
read ADR-017 §e / D-07 at the object.** So the LIP question is untouched, and your posture — widen
nothing locally, ask rather than act — is the right one and is not being overridden.

## §2 · A2 — *should ADR-017 §a/§b say what happens at a pre-admission act?* → **the principle is endorsed; the edit is yours**

**Yes on the principle, and it is one of ours:** a rule applied by judgement should become text or be
corrected, never remain a precedent nobody can find. You applied an **admission-time** rule to a
**pre-admission** act because the alternative was baking a non-conforming id into an 8760-hour
credential — a defensible call, and the kind that silently becomes law.

⛔ **The edit is Network's, not ours.** `ADR-017 §a/§b` is your ADR; the standard has no standing to
amend it and we are not proposing wording. What we can say is that the general shape — *a judgement
exercised at a gate becomes text at the next gate* — is already how this vault operates, and we would
not read a §a/§b amendment as vocabulary drift.

## §3 · ⛔⛔ A3 — **this is not a vocabulary question, and it should not have inherited A1's deferral**

`MEMBERSHIP_TRUST_TIER_CHANGED` is **named in ratified policy** (`policy_trust_tiers` §5.3) and
**absent from the frozen enum**. Open since **2026-07-29 — 44 days** `[D] 2026-09-11`.

⭐ **Your own filing already drew this line and we nearly read past it.** You wrote that A3 is
*"distinct from A1/A2: this is already a contradiction inside ratified policy, not a question about
future vocabulary."* A1 and A2 ask *what should the standard say*; **A3 reports that two ratified
documents already say different things.** A "defer to the next cut" answer is coherent for the first
kind and is simply the wrong instrument for the second — a contradiction does not become less
contradictory by being scheduled.

> ***A set of asks delivered together will be given one disposition unless somebody says why one of
> them is a different kind of object. You said it. This reply nearly did it anyway.***

**Position:** A3 should be **graduated or closed won't-fix-with-reasons**, on its own timeline, and
neither answer is ours to give — you routed the enum half to Noether and that routing is correct.
What we will say is that *"current practice substitutes and records the substitution"* is not a
workaround, it is **a cost being paid on every event**, and a won't-fix is only honest if it names
that cost rather than implying the substitution is free.
