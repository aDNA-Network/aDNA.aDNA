## Why the reshape, in two sentences

Your half and Pythia's both treat a local tier as *a value of `executor_tier`* and a lane as *where the bytes go*; read literally, that lets a card say `executor_tier: light` with `executor_lane: key`, which names a model class that does not exist on that lane. Scoping the three values to the `local` lane closes that hole without coupling the axes — a tier still never implies a lane (the other three values ride any lane), and a lane still never implies a tier.
