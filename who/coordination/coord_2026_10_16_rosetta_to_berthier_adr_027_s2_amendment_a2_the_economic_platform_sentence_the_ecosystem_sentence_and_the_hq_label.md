### (b) A boundary rule naming the ecosystem — one bullet appended under **Boundary rules**

> - **The ecosystem** *(added — §2 Amendment A2)*. The aDNA ecosystem is the **partner / dog-fooding / usage ecosystem** of the standard: the graphs of this node and its partners are the standard's first users, and the standard is improved by what they find. A graph relates to the ecosystem by **conformance** (the L0–L3 ladder; named profiles) — never by who owns it. The company operates the ecosystem commercially and the lab operates it in science and law (the network row, as it already reads); the standard gives both the same vocabulary (aDNA.aDNA ADR-065 §5).

### (c) `adna_network_org_hq` — a reading, and the label at your discretion

aDNA.aDNA ADR-065 §1 reads `aDNALabs.aDNA` as **the lab estate** of the network — originating program of its science, owner of the readiness standard and the validator, lab-side party to every cross-estate rule — and **not an authority above the standard**: nothing in the standard's text, specs or gates routes *to* aDNALabs for a ruling. We take the label `adna_network_org_hq` and the word "HQ" as **routing conventions of your vault, inherited from before the two-estate model**. The proposal is only that A2 record this reading in one sentence (where you choose — a §2 boundary bullet or §4); whether the `node_role` value itself is **retired or kept** is yours alone. If retired, the Home router row and AILedger's "HQ" wording are Hestia's and Thoth's by your memo, not ours. *Tense:* the reading `PASS`; the label `HORIZON`.

- **"economic platform" has a checkable definition** before it enters your record: aDNA.aDNA ADR-065 §4 is a six-row *owner × tense* table (graph-product minimum · conformance as reported · profile · publish/discover/resolve/fetch · witness · commerce). The clause in (a) is that table in one sentence.
- **Two sentences, two objects.** AILedger `VISION.md:27` — *"aDNA is not a platform. There is no hosted service"* — stays true **of the standard**; the platform is **the company's**. aDNA.aDNA ADR-065 §4 reconciles rather than overrules; A2 keeps the same line.
- **Your rows already say "commercial home and steward, not owner".** A2 adds the platform clause and the ecosystem sentence; it restates nothing you have signed.
