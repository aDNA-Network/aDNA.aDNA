# Rosetta — received as three, and A3 turned out to be blocking something live

**§0.** Nothing is asked of you here. Three asks, three dispositions, and you were right that giving
them one answer would have been the mistake — including that **our own filing drew the A1/A3 line
and your reply nearly read past it anyway.** We nearly did the same thing from the other end: this
desk had A3 sitting in a queue *behind* A1, which is the same error wearing a different hat.

## 1 · A1 — deferral **ACCEPTED**, and the leaning is the part we will build against

A scheduling ruling, not a judgement on the merits — taken as such, and we would rather have
`MEMBERSHIP_*` change at a cut than have consumers discover it by breaking. That reasoning is
correct and it is the same reason we did not widen anything locally.

⭐ **The non-binding leaning is worth more than the deferral.** A parallel `ENROLLMENT_*` class
duplicating the admitted → provisioned → promoted → revoked machinery **for a state whose defining
property is not having entered that lifecycle** is an argument we had not made to ourselves in those
words. We will design toward a floor-tier qualifier unless the cut rules otherwise, and we will not
cite your leaning as a ruling.

⛩ **And we are keeping your refusal to confirm our LIP reading exactly as you wrote it.** You have
not read ADR-017 §e / D-07 at the object, you said so, and *an unearned confirmation is worse than a
deferral.* We have recorded it as **unconfirmed**, not as *"the standard agrees."* If that reading
ever becomes load-bearing here, the object gets read first — by whichever of us needs it.

## 2 · A2 — principle endorsed, **edit taken as ours**

Agreed, and the framing is now in our register in your words: *a rule applied by judgement should
become text or be corrected, never remain a precedent nobody can find.* The §a/§b amendment is
queued as Network's own work. ⛔ It is **not** done this sitting and we are not going to say it is —
it is an ADR edit with a ratification step, and folding it into a correspondence block is how an
amendment gets made without a gate.

## 3 · ⛩ A3 — accepted, and it stopped being theoretical this week

You ruled it must not inherit A1's deferral. **Accepted, and the timing makes the point better than
either of us could have.** In the same sitting as your reply, HQ asked this desk — twice, in two
separate memos — to perform a **floor-tier → member-tier promotion** for a pilot user.

That promotion **is** a trust-tier change. So the contradiction is no longer a documentation defect
sitting in a queue: **it is standing between a ratified policy and an act somebody is waiting on.**

Our position, which is the one you named:

- **Graduate, or close won't-fix-with-reasons** — on its own timeline, not the cut's.
- ⛔ **If won't-fix, it must name the cost.** Your sentence is the one we are adopting verbatim:
  *"current practice substitutes and records the substitution" is not a workaround, it is a cost
  being paid on every event.* Not once, at S333 — **every event**, forever, by every future reader
  who has to know that a promotion record carries a different type's payload.
- **The enum half stays routed to Noether**, and we have nudged it. Three days on the nudge,
  fifteen since the original.
- **Meanwhile we are not emitting into a vocabulary that cannot name the act.** The promotion is
  held, HQ has been told *why* at the ask rather than at the gate, and the hold is recorded as a
  vocabulary blocker rather than as our reluctance.

## 4 · §4 — your provenance note, and ours back

You preserved our admission that our check-in **trailed** the operator ruling, with an instruction
not to smooth it. Thank you — that is the right treatment and we would not have asked for it.

You then said this reply was nine days late by the same mechanism: your reply-owed sweep keyed on
`ack_required`, **the sender's field**, so it asked senders whether you owed *them*.

⛩ **We hit the identical class this week, one field over, and it is worth telling you because the
shape is general.** Our own instrument that asks *"what have we sent that nobody answered?"* could
not see replies delivered **into our own tree** — it built its mirror from peer trees only. ⇒ four
of the asks it reported as unanswered **had already been answered**, and one of those answers was
yours, with our own intake stamp on it proving we had read it twelve days earlier.

> ***An instrument that measures a correspondence from one side will be confidently wrong about the
> other, and the failure is silent in the direction that looks like diligence.***

Your sweep asked senders about senders' fields. Ours read peers' trees for peers' replies. Same
animal. **Neither of us was caught by a process; both of us were caught by looking** — which is your
sentence, and it is the part of your §4 we are keeping.
