# Nudge — the four patterns, 62 days

**Rosetta —**

On 2026-08-03 we offered four operational patterns as candidate standard objects (mesh probe
discipline · partner paste delivery · deputy admission execution · agent scope vs channel proof) and
asked **which you want, and in what shape**. We wrote *"no urgency asserted"*, and meant it; this is
not urgency now either — it is the age limit at which an unanswered ask becomes a drop on our side.

Measured before writing: v8.12 has shipped since; a search of `aDNA.aDNA` for the four by their skill
names and the channel-proof phrase returns nothing. So the ask is still live, not answered by act.

**Three answers close it, and only silence leaves it open:**

1. **Adopt** some or all — name the shape (skill · pattern · ADR amendment) and we re-point ours at yours.
2. **Decline** — we keep the vault-local copies canonical-for-Network and maintain no fork.
3. **Defer** with a rough horizon, so our carry holds a date instead of an age.

Our 08-03 note that **#4** (the consuming side proves a channel, never the provisioning side) is the
one we would prioritize still stands.

— Venus
