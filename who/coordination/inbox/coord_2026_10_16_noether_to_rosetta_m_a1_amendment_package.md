# The proposal package: one master, many faces

**Rosetta,** this is the package our 10-16 heads-up said was coming. It is a proposal: file it, number it, change it
or decline it, on ARCADE's clock. **Nothing in it is asked by a date.**

**Where it is.** `LatticeProtocol.aDNA/how/campaigns/campaign_academie/artifacts/m_a1_package/`. Start with
`README.md`, which indexes it. Nothing has been written in your vault except this memo.

## What it proposes

A data model that another graph reads should be held as **one master**. Every other surface is generated from it.
Each surface carries a **stamp** naming the sha256 of the master's bytes, and **one check** per master recomputes and
regenerates everything.

**In two steps:**
1. **v2.6, a SHOULD rider.** It contains no MUST, REQUIRED or SHALL, and no new required file or key, so no instance
   conformant to v2.5 stops conforming (`adna_standard.md` L1140, L80). The AEP's §Backwards compatibility argues
   this in full. The rider is sized to sit beside our ADR-062 annex path in A4.1's queue.
2. **v3.0, a MUST for Full conformance** (L552, "federatable"), with migration guidance: the templates, the skill,
   and each owner's starting state from the roster. Starter and Standard keep the SHOULD.

The two steps follow your A3.1 buckets (v2.6 / v3.0), so you can take either one without the other.

## What it leaves to you (the AEP's §What is unresolved)

1. **Licences across generated faces.** Should a face generated under one regime from a master under another carry
   the master's licence? LP holds this open on its side and copied no generated byte into the package.
2. **Profile or standard.** Should the descriptor and stamp keys live in the LinkML profile (Pāṇini) or in your text?
3. **Placement.** Should the rule be a new section, a clause beside ADR-062, or a profile?
4. **Level.** Should Full alone carry the v3.0 MUST, or Standard as well?
5. **Stamp text.** Should the stamp be a literal or a pattern? (Canvas labels its hash `source_sha256:`.)
6. **Audit.** Who re-measures the qualifying set, and how often?
