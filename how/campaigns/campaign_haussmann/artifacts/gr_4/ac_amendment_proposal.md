## 0. Why this pass ran, and why its aim is different

Every one of the nine prior passes was aimed at a **build** mission, where the characteristic defect
is *a stated method that cannot move a stated test*. **GR-4 is the campaign's first CONTENT-class
mission to reach this gate**, and a content mission fails differently:

## 3. The five defects

### ⭐⭐ DEFECT-1 — AC-4 IS TESTED ONLY BY ITS CONSTRAINT, NEVER BY ITS SUBSTANCE (found V→AC)

AC-4 has two halves: *the disambiguation **reaches** a first-contact surface* (substance) and *the
receiving surface's FKGL **stays under target*** (constraint). **V3 is the reading census. It tests
the constraint. Nothing tests the substance.**

⇒ **The mission could ship nothing at all, run the census, and V3 passes** — reading level is
trivially unchanged when no copy lands. The one criterion whose entire point is *"content exists
where a reader will meet it"* has **no presence limb anywhere in V1–V6**.

⭐ Worse in one specific way, and it is why this is the sharpest finding here: **FKGL falls as prose
gets shorter and simpler.** A one-line disambiguation *lowers* the number. So V3 does not merely
fail to test AC-4 — **it moves in the reassuring direction when AC-4 is met**, which is the
signature of a limb that will be read as confirmation. *P4.1's structural gap inverted, for the
fifth time in this campaign.*

**Remedy — no new instrument** (conventions 15/16/17): V3 gains a **presence assertion** on the
ruled receiving surface, measured on the **`.md` twin** (the surface whose verb is *"a reader
encounters"*, per convention 17's amendment), with the before-figure recorded in the same artifact.

### DEFECT-2 — V2's AC-6 ASSIGNMENT IS VACUOUS BY CONSTRUCTION

V2 is a *planned*-framing probe over **new forward-looking sentences**. AC-6's **met-state is that
nothing ships**. ⇒ V2 has nothing to iterate and **passes because the set is empty** — it certifies
a mechanism it never exercised, which is B0's *"a control that passes for the wrong reason"*
arriving in a limb label.

**Remedy, free:** strike AC-6 from V2's `[asserts]` list. **AC-6 is V6's alone** — a dated absence
assertion naming its surface (P4.3's F-v precedent: *a deferral recorded only in narrative is a
deferral with no gate*).

### DEFECT-3 — AC-2 IS SATISFIABLE BY A MENTION RATHER THAN A DOCTRINE LAYER

AC-2's baseline is **0** occurrences of `ADR-016`/`token_budget` in `site/src/**` `[D]`. V1 is a
content probe. ⇒ **a single passing reference — in a changelog entry, a glossary stub, anywhere —
takes the count from 0 to 1 and V1 goes green**, while the *doctrine layer* AC-2 exists to publish
remains unwritten.

⚠ And note the asymmetry with AC-1, **same limb, same sitting**: AC-1's exclusion set is spelled out
and asserted; AC-2 got none. **A criterion and its sibling under one limb are two objects, and
hardening one is not hardening the other** — P4.4b's FINDING, second sighting.

**Remedy:** AC-2 names its **home pages** (`mission-decomposition.mdx` / `design-a-mission.mdx`) and
a **substance floor** derived from the existing doctrine sections on those pages — never a bare
count, and never a floor typed by feel (B0: *a number written by feel is a formality wearing a pin's
clothing*).

### DEFECT-4 — V4's SINGLE MUTATION CANNOT ATTRIBUTE (GR-3's clause, applied before the harness exists)

V4 asserts **two** things — nav is exactly 7, and the homepage entry point is present — and is
red-proven by **one** mutation: adding an 8th nav entry. That mutation reds via the **nav-count**
assertion. ⇒ **the entry-point assertion would never have been demonstrated to fail.**

⭐ This is **`F-z` verbatim** — *convention 14's missing second clause: **a demonstration is only
worth what it can attribute***, minted at GR-3 one sitting ago, when `console_clean_redtest.sh`
reported 5/5 while wrong about which assertion case 3 exercised. **Applied here at authoring time
rather than discovered in the fourteenth day**, which is the first time this campaign has spent that
finding forward instead of paying for it.

**Remedy:** V4 carries **one mutation per assertion**, each case **naming the assertion it reds
via**, and a red via the wrong assertion reports as a **HARNESS BUG**, not a pass.

### DEFECT-5 — V5 CAN SEE A ROW THAT EXISTS, NEVER A ROW THAT IS MISSING

AC-7 requires *every* new public sentence to carry a claim-register row. V5 asserts *"register rows
present for every new claim"* — but nothing **enumerates the new claims**, so V5 confirms presence
of what it was handed and is structurally blind to an omission.

⇒ **the over-masking shape** (B0's FINDING 4: *masks only ever grow*) in a governance limb: the set

touched content files), and the enumeration itself is asserted — gate-48's ratified discipline,
