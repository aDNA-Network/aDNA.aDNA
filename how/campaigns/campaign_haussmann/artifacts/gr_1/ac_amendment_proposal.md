## 2 · The findings

### ⛔ FAIL-1 — AC-1's only limb can be vacuously green, and its own "red before the fix" clause may be unsatisfiable

`AC-1 × V1`, found **AC→V**; recurs at `AC-1 × V6`.

AC-1 requires that no font ship as a `data:` URI. V1 tests that a **headers-applied probe records zero
blocked font loads**. Those are not the same claim, and the gap is not academic:

- The inlined asset is a **JetBrains Mono `cyrillic-ext`** subset (~2028 B) `[D]`.
- `BaseLayout.astro:52-54` preloads **latin subsets only** — its own comment says so `[D]`.
- A subset that is neither preloaded nor matched by a painted glyph is never loaded ⇒ **never blocked**
  ⇒ **V1 is green on the unfixed tree**, and green again after the fix. A limb that cannot distinguish
  the two states is not a test.
- ⇒ V1's *"demonstrated RED on the pre-fix tree"* clause, and V6's requirement that the limb be
  red-proven, may both be **unsatisfiable** — which would leave AC-1 permanently unmeetable.

⚠ **This does not contradict the revue, and the tension is the finding.** The revue captured **real
console text** `[D]` — *"Loading the font 'data:font/woff2;base64,…' violates … `font-src 'self'`"* —
and characterised it as firing **on every page, both themes**. Both observations are `[D]`; they cannot
both be complete. Either the *"every page"* scope is wider than the sample, or an engine materialises a
`data:`-URI face eagerly because there is no network fetch to defer.

⭐ **The point is that a criterion must not be keyed to a count nobody derived.** The revue's *"every
page"* is a **typed** figure (KW-14). Written against it, AC-1 is unfalsifiable-green if the true count
is zero on latin pages and wrongly-red otherwise.

### ⭐⭐ FAIL-1 — CORRECTED AT O1 (2026-08-28, appended dated; the analysis above retained, SO-6)

**The factual half of FAIL-1 was wrong, and V1's first run falsified it.** Measured with the
production CSP applied to a pre-fix build: **50 of 50 page×theme loads refused the font** — *every
page, both themes*, precisely as the revue recorded. Post-fix: **0 of 50**.

⇒ **The revue's figure was right.** This desk's inference — that an un-preloaded, glyph-gated subset
would rarely or never load — does not hold for an inlined face: **`unicode-range` defers a NETWORK
FETCH, and a `data:` URI has no fetch to defer**, so the engine constructs the face immediately and
CSP is evaluated at construction. The lazy-loading intuition is real and simply does not reach this
case.

⭐ **The remedy stands, and why it stands is the interesting part.** V1 *would* have worked as a limb
after all — its "red before the fix" clause was satisfiable all along. But the static limb is still
the one to gate on, for the half of the argument that survived: a `data:` URI in built CSS is
**present or absent**, so it cannot be vacuous, and it does not depend on a browser's font-loading
behaviour remaining what it is today. ⇒ **A correct remedy reached from a wrong premise is still a
wrong premise**, and it is corrected here rather than quietly inherited by whoever cites this
document next.

⚠ **The meta-point holds in the other direction too.** The revue's *"every page"* was a **typed**
figure when written (KW-14) — right, but not derived. This run makes it derived. *A typed figure that
happens to be correct is still typed*, and measuring is the only way to tell the two apart.

**Remedy — zero new instruments, and it makes the criterion decidable either way.** AC-1 gains a
**static limb that is directly checkable and cannot be vacuous**: *no `data:font` occurrence in built
CSS*, asserted over `dist/**/*.css`, red-proven by restoring the inline threshold. V1's dynamic probe
is **retained and demoted to corroboration**, and its red run becomes the instrument that **settles the
scope question** rather than a precondition of passing. **The fix is identical under either reading** —
`assetsInlineLimit: 0` removes the `data:` URI, so there is nothing left to block.

### ⛔ GAP-1 — AC-2's P2-3 half is covered by no limb

`AC-2 × V2`, found **V→AC**.

AC-2 has two halves: the **llms.txt verb correction + six-emitter sweep**, and the **P2-3 staged
upstream memo**. V2 measures *emitters over built output*. **A memo is not an emitter.** So AC-2 can be
ticked with the memo unwritten, or written to nowhere.

This is **P4.1's structural gap** (an output covered by no criterion) inverted into **P4.5b's** form (a
criterion covered by no limb) — the shape the reverse direction exists to catch.

**Remedy — zero new instruments, and the campaign already owns the mechanism.** P4.3's **F-v**
precedent: *a deferral recorded only in narrative is a deferral with no gate.* The memo half is
discharged as a **register row with a named destination**, and V2 gains one clause asserting that row
exists. No reviewer, no checker, no fifth instrument at a sitting's tail.

### ⛔ GAP-2 — AC-4 asserts three things and V4 tests one; the untested one is the load-bearing one

`AC-4 × V4`, found **V→AC**.

AC-4 requires (a) the pin resolves **publicly** in the repo `source_repo` names, (b) the generator reads
the **upstream** SHA rather than local HEAD, and (c) **byte-identity re-measured at that SHA**. V4
asserts **(a) only**.

⭐ **(c) is the half that matters most**, and the reason is AC-4's whole purpose: a pin that resolves
publicly but whose bytes do not match is **worse than a dead link**, because the page explicitly invites
the reader to `shasum -a 256` their own clone and diff. A live link to the wrong bytes converts a broken
affordance into a **false one**. Note that gate-36's existing byte test compares committed bytes to
*manifest hashes* — a local-vs-local check that says nothing about the upstream SHA.

⚠ **And the revue's mitigation cannot be inherited as evidence.** It measured the four files
byte-identical to public **`raw/main`**. `main` is **not the pin**, and `main` moves — convention 15's
pin-supersession face, applied inward.

**Remedy — measured at this gate, and it resolves to a recorded fact rather than an instrument.** See
the control in §2's last entry: all four files are byte-identical between local `.adna` HEAD and
**upstream `v8.9`** `[D]`. So (c) is **already true** and is discharged by *recording the measurement
with its supersession condition on the criterion's face* — not by a build-time network check, which
would make CI depend on GitHub availability. AC-4 gains the supersession clause; **V4 gains nothing**.

### ⚠ DEFECT-4 — V6's `[asserts]` list omits two of the criteria it actually governs

`AC-2 × V6` and `AC-5 × V6`, found **V→AC**.

V6 requires every gate this mission repairs or adds to be red-proven by mutation, and labels itself
`[asserts AC-1, AC-3, AC-4]`. But **V2 is a new assertion** and **V5 is a set of new assertions** —
both are things this mission adds, both are red-provable by mutation, and neither is named.

⭐ **The label is exactly the field P4.4b's FINDING 7 made mandatory**, on the ground that *a pass cannot
ask "is this criterion tested by anything" against a field that never says what anything tests.* GR-1
carried the labels correctly on V1–V5 and **under-scoped the one limb that is about all of them** — a
label can be present, well-formed, and still wrong, which is the harder failure to see because its
presence reads as diligence. *(Same shape as convention 17's amendment: naming a surface does not make
it the right one.)*

**Remedy — free.** `V6 … [asserts AC-1, AC-2, AC-3, AC-4, AC-5]`.

### ⚠ DEFECT-5 — one of AC-5's "three disagreeing surfaces" never renders, so V5 cannot assert it

`AC-5 × V5`, found **AC→V**.

AC-5 requires the v2/v3 contradiction resolved *"applied to ALL THREE disagreeing surfaces."* The three
are `visual-identity-v2.mdx:4` (`ref_title`), `writing-guidelines.mdx:19` + `:176` (two links), and
**`HomeHero.astro:327`** — which is a **source comment**: *"surrounding chrome keeps v2 tokens until
visual-identity-v3."* A comment is stripped from built output (`gate-28` exists to guarantee exactly
that). ⇒ **V5, which asserts strings in built output, structurally cannot see it.**

⭐ **And the correct reading inverts its role.** P4.2's lesson — *a migration announced in a comment is
not a migration* — applies in reverse here: the comment is not a surface to fix, it is **the strongest
evidence for how the ruling should go**, because it states that v2 tokens are what actually ship.

**Remedy — scope shrinks.** AC-5 names **two rendering surfaces** to correct; the comment is
reclassified as **input to the ⛩ ruling**, cited in the ruling's record. If the ruling makes the comment
stale, it is updated as ordinary hygiene, not as a criterion limb.
