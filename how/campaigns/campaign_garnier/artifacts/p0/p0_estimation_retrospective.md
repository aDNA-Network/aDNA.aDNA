## Why the forecast failed

- [I] The70kT baseline forecast did not price two full independent instrument readers plus coordinator collection. The two accepted scorers alone were about140kT.
- [D/I] Both initial scorer contexts crossed into historical instrument material. Rejection and fresh replacement were correct but avoidably expensive; their combined effort is retained, not erased.
- [D/I] Broad template captures, reference access failures, complementary scanners and the fresh-load diagram investigation needed more orchestration than the forecast allowed. Bounded collection does not equal complete semantic assessment.
- [D/I] Private governance primed one synthetic reader; thread limits then required public-only contexts outside the vault. The scientist’s long navigation exposed discoverability questions but cannot simulate a three-minute human limit.

## Changes to the estimation method

[I] Price each independent scorer/reader explicitly, with its actual permitted corpus and image set. Supply bounded normative excerpts and perform the isolation check before expensive reads. For synthetic navigation, predeclare a page/interaction allowance as well as the absence of human timing; do not let a long search masquerade as quick comprehension. Reuse frozen artifacts instead of repeating collection unless source or instrument changes. Keep scope failures and tool limits visible before spending another context.

[I] **DP2 proposal: P1 reforecast 320kT** instead of 186, derived from additive proposed fields in its three mission cards (150/90/80). Original budgets remain intact and uncommitted. P1.1 explicitly prices its three fresh prescreens and two graders; P1.2 prices source-tour reconciliation/clean execution; P1.3 prices four surfaces and formative feedback disposition. Three lead sittings remain a forecast, not a cap or a count of independent contexts. Human recruitment/waiting is separate.

[I] Recommend committing320 at DP2 with a spend/scope review after each mission, before authorizing any next-phase expansion. If the operator retains186, begin only a bounded P1.1 increment and re-estimate remaining scope at its close; do not silently drop controls, source fidelity, accessibility or humans to fit the number. Future midpoint/final scoring budgets need the same explicit-context reforecast at their own gates. No later phase budget is changed here.
