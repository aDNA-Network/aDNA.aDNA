# Decisive readers

[D] Bench checked: all sixteen reviewer files and the reviewer routing index; historical examples in those files are not present site facts. [I] These are sharpened audience briefs over existing lenses, as WADNA did, so no new reviewer file is justified. The Enterprise Architect is an adopter lens, not a seventeenth reviewer.

## Senior / frontier engineer

[I] Existing lenses: Movement Skeptic, Performance Engineer, Standard Archivist and Enterprise Architect adopter. Three seconds: **Is this a competent, usable convention?** Three minutes: **Would I clone it, and can I check its claims?** Convincing evidence: A minimal working artifact, exact command, source-linked tests and limitations. Reject unsupported adjectives, inflated affiliations and a metaphor substituting for a definition. Route: home → get-started → reference/specification.

## VC / foundation program officer

[I] Existing lenses: Brand Strategist, Movement Skeptic and Lab/Org Steward adopter. Three seconds: **What exists, and who stands behind it?** Three minutes: **What could my support change, and what remains founder-operated?** Convincing evidence: Dated operational state, consent-qualified people, public work and a concrete contribution path. Reject unsupported adjectives, inflated affiliations and a metaphor substituting for a definition. Route: home → about → state-of-the-network.

## AI scientist / researcher

[I] Existing lenses: Standard Archivist, Movement Skeptic and Researcher adopter. Three seconds: **Is there a technical idea I can inspect?** Three minutes: **What is the mechanism, prior art and reproducible evidence?** Convincing evidence: A precise definition, a real governed example, prior-art links and an explicit evaluation boundary. Reject unsupported adjectives, inflated affiliations and a metaphor substituting for a definition. Route: home → get-started → reference/specification.

## Standing readers and human protocol

[I] Clinician/researcher, OSS contributor and autonomous-agent paths remain mandatory regression checks. Shared cultural heritage is an ethical thesis; private, patient, proprietary or non-consented data does not thereby become public. Keep the local-files and opt-in sharing distinction explicit. Democracy and freedom describe the invitation and aspirations; show actual governance powers separately.

[I] Operator recruits at least five humans per decisive class. At 3 seconds ask for category/mechanism; at 3 minutes ask what exists, what is planned, what they would do next and what evidence they found. No coaching. Require ≥80% of each class to answer each window correctly under a rubric sealed before recruitment; publish denominators and dissent, not just averages. Keep the standing-reader results separate. No agent recruitment or outreach is authorized.
