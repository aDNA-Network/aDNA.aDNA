## Accepted amendments and owners

1. **Visible first deliverable — P1.1.** Produce a reviewable low-fidelity homepage/storyboard with a clear mechanism, actual file/code example and obvious next action. Keep discovery bounded to the changed surfaces and frozen P0 findings. Preserve semantic reading order, equivalent text and readable controls while changing hierarchy.
2. **Public-good identity — P1.1 and P1.3.** Retain Agentic DNA; use “AI DNA” as an accessible explanatory bridge after the concrete mechanism. Connect shared cultural inheritance to knowledge people choose to share, democratic participation and public benefit. Distinguish current stewardship from future collective governance. Do not imply all data is common property or that private context must be public; keep the explanation in accessible text rather than art alone.
3. **Finding ownership — P1.3 / P2.2 / P3.3.** P1.3 owns FG-P0-002 on /privacy and /state-of-the-network, including HTML/twin agreement and claims limited to observed telemetry behavior; P4.1 retains actual collector/field verification. P3.3 explicitly owns FG-P0-003 on /learn/concepts/triad, with legible labels on fresh mobile loads in both themes and an equivalent text/keyboard path. Rosetta in P2.2 owns the seven FG-P0-001 source/HTML/twin comparisons, with P2.3 supplying corpus review and GR-7 retaining predecessor integration. Stale public counts block release until verified on the authorized release candidate and subsequently the deployed surface.
4. **Evidence limits and gate adoption — P1.1.** P0 is accepted for planning, not as proof of comparative rank, human comprehension or field performance. Preserve the frozen pack and excluded attempts. Before any experimental check becomes an approval gate, demonstrate failure for empty/incomplete/duplicate capture populations and actual blocking vocabulary violations, including nonzero process exit. An advisory census stays explicitly advisory. Add positive controls and red-tests together, early in the implementation sitting; reusable provider work follows the federation wrapper and remains locally staged. No whole-baseline rerun is required by this amendment.
5. **Workload discipline — all P1 missions.** Commit 150 + 90 + 80 = 320 kT as the working forecast; preserve original 62 + 61 + 63 = 186 kT. Include independent reviewer work and failed attempts once. Freeze reviewer inputs, route limits and stop conditions before runs; reuse P0 and do not repeat the full reference cohort. At each mission close report rough actuals, uncertainty and remaining phase forecast; surface projected envelope/scope excess before expanding work. These are content-load estimates, not billing amounts or measured calibration.
6. **Human checkpoint — P1.3 / DP3.** Operator-supplied formative feedback from at least one engineer, funder and scientist precedes DP3 and detailed visual production. Preserve individual confusions and correction/disposition, without a success percentage from this small panel. Synthetic answers cannot discharge the human requirement. Agents do not recruit or contact participants.
